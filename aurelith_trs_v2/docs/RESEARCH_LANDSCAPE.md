# Research Landscape

Research pass: 2026-09-19.

## Letta / MemGPT

Letta already provides persistent agent identity, memory blocks, recall memory,
external memory, and portable long-running state.

Implication: **persistent memory alone is not novel**.

Sources:
- https://github.com/letta-ai/letta
- https://github.com/letta-ai/letta-code

## Generative Agents

Generative Agents demonstrated memory, retrieval, reflection, and planning.

Implication: **memory + reflection + planning alone is not novel**.

Source:
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442

## OpenCog / Hyperon

OpenCog/Hyperon explores cognitive architecture and symbolic / graph-based
knowledge processing.

Implication: the LLM should be one reasoning component, not Aurelith's entire
identity.

Sources:
- https://opencog.org/
- https://github.com/opencog

## Consciousness indicators

Butlin et al. proposed evaluating AI against computational indicator properties
derived from recurrent processing, global workspace, higher-order theories,
predictive processing, and attention-schema theory.

Source:
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708

## Aurelith/TRS novelty target

The defensible research target is the combination of:

- temporal cognitive-state lineage
- evidence-linked self-model revision
- identity inertia
- bounded workspace competition
- contradiction pressure
- calibrated uncertainty
- predictive world modeling
- multi-timescale consolidation
- longitudinal evaluation

Novelty must be established through literature review and experimental
comparison, not simply asserted.
